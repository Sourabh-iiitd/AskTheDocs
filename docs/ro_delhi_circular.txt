UIDAI Regional Office Delhi - Internship Circular 2026
File No. RO-DEL-21037/1/2026 (E-23191). Dated 08.10.2026.
Subject: Inviting applications for internship in the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) at Regional Office, Delhi, for the year 2026.

UIDAI Regional Office Delhi invites applications from eligible candidates for internship. The number of interns to be engaged in Delhi:
1. Technical wing: 2 interns. Qualification: B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, Software Engineering, Data Science, IT, Electronics and other similar technical fields. Location: UIDAI Regional Office Delhi.
2. Non-Technical wing: 1 intern. Qualification: MBA/PGDM. Location: UIDAI Regional Office Delhi.

How to apply: Interested candidates may submit their application in the prescribed format (Annexure-A of the Internship Policy) along with their CV on the email ids dd2.rodelhi@uidai.net.in and asoadmin.rodelhi@uidai.net.in. No physical applications for internship will be entertained.
For applicants who are currently enrolled in an institute, the application needs to be sponsored or forwarded by the institution in the prescribed format (Annexure-C of the Internship Policy).
The last date of receipt of application is 08.12.2026 (8 December 2026).
